How it works

1 · Connect
Click Connect project folder and choose the net_zero_desk folder (Chrome / Edge). Images are then saved straight into assets/…. Without it you can download a ZIP instead.
2 · Generate
On any palette below, press Copy prompt, paste it into your image generator, and save the single contact-sheet picture it makes.
3 · Drop
Drag the palette picture (or paste with Ctrl+V) onto its drop zone. It is split into individual images, named and checked.
4 · Save
Fix the grid if needed, untick bad cells, press Save. The game uses the new image automatically and keeps the SVG as a fall-back.
